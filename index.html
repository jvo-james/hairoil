How much is one bottle?

Can I use it on my hair type?

The brand describes the oil as suitable for all hair types. If you have a sensitive scalp, check the full ingredient list and consider a patch test before regular use.

How do I use the oil?

Part your hair into smaller sections, apply the oil then massage gently on your scalp.
